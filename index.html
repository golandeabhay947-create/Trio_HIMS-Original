ABOUT TRIO HIMS

Healthcare Automation with TRIO HIMS

A centralized hospital information management platform connecting clinical, diagnostic, financial and administrative operations.

One platform for connected hospital operations

TRIO HIMS brings different hospital workflows together so teams can access the information they need through an integrated system.

  • Centralized patient information and hospital records.
  • Integrated clinical and diagnostic workflows.
  • Financial and administrative management.
  • Reporting and analytics for hospital operations.
  • Support for multiple hospital departments through connected modules.

The platform is designed to simplify routine operational tasks while improving access to relevant information across departments.

    TRIO HIMS is a Hospital Information Management System designed to help hospitals manage clinical, diagnostic, financial and operational workflows through an integrated platform.

QUICK TOUR

Take a Quick Product Tour

A step-by-step walkthrough of the end-to-end patient and operational lifecycle in TRIO HIMS.

STEP 01 — FRONT DESK & ADMISSION

Digital Patient Registration

Capture comprehensive patient demographics, emergency contacts, insurance eligibility, and triage notes in seconds. Generates unique UHID barcode tags that automatically synchronize with all hospital modules.

UHID Generation Insurance Capture Barcode Tagging Queue Allocation
STEP 02 — CLINICAL CONSULTATION

Doctor Consultation & EHR

Doctors access unified patient medical histories, vitals, allergy alerts, past diagnoses, and digital clinical templates. Prescriptions, lab investigation requests, and radiology orders are sent directly across departments.

Unified EHR Digital Prescriptions Diagnostic Orders Clinical Decision Support
STEP 03 — DIAGNOSTICS & LAB

Integrated LIMS & RIS Workflows

Sample collection with barcoding, automated analyzer machine interfacing, test verification, and immediate report publishing. Doctors receive real-time notifications for critical parameter alerts.

Analyzer Interfacing Barcode Tracking Doctor Verification Critical Value Alerts
STEP 04 — PHARMACY & STORES

Connected Pharmacy Operations

Prescriptions instantly display in the pharmacy dispensing queue. Real-time batch-level inventory tracking, expiry alerts, and automatic deduction prevent stockouts and medication errors.

E-Prescription Queue Batch & Expiry Control Inventory Deduction Direct Counter Billing
STEP 05 — BILLING & DISCHARGE

Consolidated Hospital Billing

Automated charge aggregation from consultation, investigations, medicines, procedures, and bed occupancy. Transparent invoicing with TPA/cash handling and zero revenue leakage.

Centralized Billing Insurance & TPA Audit Trail Tally Integration
STEP 06 — MANAGEMENT INSIGHTS

Administrative Reports & Analytics

Executive dashboards track hospital KPIs, bed occupancy rates, department turnaround times, doctor OPD statistics, and revenue insights for data-driven administrative decisions.

Real-time KPIs Occupancy Metrics Revenue Reports Statutory Compliance
